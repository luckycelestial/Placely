# Reports & Analytics

## Purpose
The Reports & Analytics screen is where admins review placement performance, trends, outcomes, and decision-making dashboards.

## User role
- Placement Staff.
- Placement Admin.
- College Super Admin.

## Navigation placement
- Admin desktop shell uses a right-side sidebar.
- Admin mobile shell uses bottom navigation or drawer behavior where needed.
- This is a strategic insight screen in the admin shell.

## Screen goal
This screen should help the placement team understand what is happening across the season, what has improved, what is lagging, and what needs intervention.

## Core sections
- KPI summary cards.
- Year-wise analytics.
- Department-wise analytics.
- Placement funnel view.
- Company yield analytics.
- Student risk or intervention indicators.
- Charts and trend panels.
- Export or report controls.

## Key actions
- Filter reports by year, department, or drive.
- Open a detailed trend view.
- Export data or charts.
- Review funnel stages.
- Compare companies or departments.
- Identify students or segments needing support.

## Content rules
- Focus on decision-grade analytics, not vanity metrics.
- Show funnel and outcome trends clearly.
- Make charts readable and connected to action.
- Keep the reporting useful for placement season decisions.

## States
- No historical data.
- Partial batch data.
- Full yearly trend data.
- Export-ready report state.
- Saved view state.

## Data needed
- Placement totals.
- Funnel stages.
- Department outcomes.
- Year-wise performance.
- Company outcomes.
- Student risk indicators.
- Export metadata.

## UX notes
- Use charts and tables together.
- Support saved filters and recurring report views.
- Make the highest-value insights visible first.
- Keep it fast enough for repeated staff use.
