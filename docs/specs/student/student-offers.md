# Offers & Joining

## Purpose
The Offers & Joining screen is where students track final offers, acceptance status, verification steps, joining dates, and any post-offer actions required before they join a company.

## User role
- Student only.

## Navigation placement
- Student desktop shell uses sidebar navigation.
- Student mobile shell uses bottom navigation.
- This is a critical post-selection workflow screen.

## Screen goal
This screen should make the full offer-to-joining lifecycle clear and actionable, not just show that a student was selected.

## Core sections
- Offer summary cards.
- Offer status and acceptance state.
- Joining date and reporting details.
- Verification or mismatch alerts.
- Required next actions.
- Post-offer lifecycle status.

## Key actions
- View offer details.
- Accept or acknowledge offer.
- Upload or provide proof if required.
- Review joining instructions.
- Track verification status.
- Mark joining progress or completion.

## Content rules
- Show company, role, CTC, and joining information clearly.
- Highlight any missing steps or validation issues.
- Keep selected, verified, joined, deferred, and dropped states distinct.
- Make this screen feel official and final-stage oriented.

## States
- Offer received.
- Offer accepted.
- Verification pending.
- Joining scheduled.
- Joined.
- Deferred or mismatch flagged.
- No offer yet.

## Data needed
- Company name.
- Role title.
- CTC or package.
- Offer status.
- Acceptance date.
- Joining date.
- Verification details.
- Required documents or proof.
- Final outcome.

## UX notes
- Treat each offer as an active lifecycle object.
- Keep critical warnings visible.
- Use clear status labels and action prompts.
- Avoid reducing this screen to a simple offer list.

## Related screens

- My Applications.
- My Progress / ATS Timeline.
- Notifications / Task Inbox.
