# Job Opportunities / Drives

## Purpose
The Job Opportunities / Drives screen is where students discover available placement drives, understand eligibility, and open a drive in detail before applying. It should feel personalized, transparent, and action-oriented.

## User role
- Student only.

## Navigation placement
- Student desktop shell uses sidebar navigation.
- Student mobile shell uses bottom navigation.
- This is one of the main discovery screens in the student shell.

## Screen goal
This screen should help students quickly find the drives that matter to them, understand why they are eligible or not eligible, and take action from a drive detail view.

## Core sections
- Search bar.
- Filter bar.
- Recommended drives.
- All published drives list.
- Drive cards with eligibility badges.
- Drive detail panel or page.
- Apply CTA and status indicator.

## Key actions
- Search drives by company, role, location, or skill.
- Filter by eligibility, department, year, CTC, drive type, and application status.
- Open a drive for full details.
- Apply to a drive.
- View why eligible or why not eligible explanation.
- Save or revisit a drive later.

## Content rules
- Show eligible and recommended drives first.
- Be transparent about eligibility rules.
- Make the application state visible on every relevant card.
- Keep the list readable with clear badges and compact information.

## States
- No drives available.
- Some drives available but student is not eligible for most.
- Eligible and recommended drives available.
- Applied state.
- Closed or expired drive state.

## Data needed
- Company name.
- Role title.
- CTC or package.
- Drive type.
- Location.
- Drive dates.
- Eligibility rules.
- Application window.
- Drive status.
- Personalized eligibility result.

## UX notes
- Use cards for quick scanning and a detail view for the full drive.
- Make eligibility explanation easy to understand.
- Keep filters strong enough for serious use.
- Avoid making this feel like a generic notice board.

## Related screens
- Student Dashboard.
- My Applications.
- Offers & Joining.
- Notifications / Task Inbox.
