Design System

Overview

Timespent uses a bright, airy, and highly polished interface with a playful app-store aesthetic. The visual tone feels friendly and energetic, combining bold typography, rounded containers, and vivid red accents to communicate focus, momentum, and approachability.

Colors

  • Primary/Accent (#ff383c): vivid red used for key CTAs, highlights, outlines, charts, and playful emphasis details.
  • Background (#fcfcfc): near-white background that keeps the layout open, clean, and minimal.
  • Text (#1f1f1f): deep charcoal used for headlines, body text, icons, and most borders.
  • Card/Surface Accent (#dc143c): stronger crimson used for illustrated cards and some elevated surfaces.
  • Pure Black (#111111): used sparingly for button text, shadows, and the darkest icon details.
  • Highlight Yellow (#ffd400): marker-style highlight used behind emphasized copy.
  • Neutral Gray (#e6e6e6): subtle dividers, device chrome details, and secondary UI structure.
  • White (#ffffff): used for device screens, button fills, and inset card areas.

Typography

  • Headlines: Bricolage Grotesque, mostly bold and display-oriented. H1 is large and heavy for hero statements, while H2–H4 stay in the same family with lighter weights and generous line heights for a soft, contemporary feel.
  • Body: Bricolage Grotesque, regular weight, typically around 20px with relaxed line height for easy reading and a friendly tone.
  • Labels/Captions: Small UI labels and button text use a compact system sans stack visually similar to Apple UI typography, set at about 14px with semibold weight.
  • The overall typographic style is rounded, modern, and slightly whimsical rather than strictly geometric or corporate.

Components

  • Buttons: Large pill-like buttons with prominent padding, thin dark borders, and strong drop shadows. Primary buttons use a vivid red fill with dark text and feel tactile and app-like; secondary buttons are mostly transparent or white with a simple border and less visual weight.
  • Cards: Rounded rectangles with 24px corner radius, thin dark outlines, soft shadows, and light inner surfaces. Cards often feel layered and floating, with illustrated content or chart UI inside.
  • Inputs: Inputs are not prominently visible, but the style implied by the system is minimal, lightly bordered, and rounded with a clean white or near-white fill and subtle focus emphasis.
  • Icon/UI chips: Small floating chips and tabs use rounded capsules, dark or white fills, thin borders, and occasional colored edge accents that add playful motion and hierarchy.

Spacing & Elevation

Spacing is generous and breathable, with large vertical gaps between hero sections and content blocks. The scale appears to move in clear steps from tight UI padding to very large marketing whitespace, creating a calm, premium feel. Elevation is soft and layered: small shadows for chips and buttons, medium shadows for primary surfaces, and a consistent slight lift rather than heavy depth.

Do's and Don'ts

  • Do: use large, confident headlines with strong contrast and generous spacing.
  • Do: keep the interface clean and mostly white, letting red accents carry the energy.
  • Do: use rounded corners and soft shadows to make components feel friendly and tactile.
  • Do: favor simple borders and minimal decoration over complex gradients or heavy effects.
  • Don't: introduce cool-toned or muted accent colors that dilute the brand’s energetic red identity.
  • Don't: use sharp, boxy, or overly technical UI shapes that fight the playful app-like feel.
